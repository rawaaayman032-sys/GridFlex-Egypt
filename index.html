GridFlex Egypt is a smart digital platform that helps users optimize their electricity usage by aligning flexible demand with periods of higher renewable energy availability.

The Problem

Egypt's energy grid challenges

Egypt continues to depend on fossil fuels for electricity generation while facing challenges in integrating renewable energy sources, especially solar and wind, into the national grid.

  • 1 Continued dependence on fossil fuels & natural gas.
  • 2 Difficulty integrating intermittent renewable energy into the grid.
  • 3 Mismatch between renewable availability and consumer demand peaks.

The Solution

Demand-side flexibility platform

GridFlex Egypt bridges supply and demand by forecasting solar/wind availability and automatically shifting flexible appliance loads to clean energy peak windows.
